Does the land have an ugly face to it but a beautiful “body”? My best land was horrific looking on its face, and hard to access
thru the briars, but had 2 access points and is utterly beautiful and very valuable, look for “ugly faced” land! People are lazy, if
they see UGLY FACED land, hard to get thru and walk thru, they give up!!! DON’T GIVE UP OR BE LAZY. Killer land can often
have a very UGLY FACE to it!

The BEST TIMES to hunt for land is when the weather and conditions are bad, PEOPLE ARE LAZY AS HELL, look for newly
listed land in the winter, or wet season!!!! AWESOME ADVICE AND I ASSURE YOU IT WORKS!

VERY IMPORTANT! CHECK FOR YOUR PROPERTY TAXES ON THE LAND that you’re thinking of buying!

Has there been a survey on the land??? Its worth its weight in GOLD!! However people being so lazy, they’ll often avoid buying
land WITHOUT a survey, but my best deals are unsurveyed land. If it’s a lot of land, WHO GIVES A DAMN if the survey comes
up, say on 100 acres, 5 acres SHORT, you got it so cheap per acre, you’re still sitting ON GOLD !! I bought 75 unsurveyed acres
for $234 an acre. If, when my survey is done, it comes up 4 acres or so short, I DON’T GIVE A DAMN!!!! I got it all for a steal and
a half! THINK!!!

A PROFESSIONAL LAND SURVEY IS WORTH ITS WEIGHT IN GOLD, but you can get a KILLER DEAL ON land that has not been
surveyed because people are suspicious and or lazy on unsurveyed land, just make sure it’s a DEAL!

ONLY TALK TO NEIGHBORS AFTER YOU MAKE THE BUY, avoid being seen by neighbors of the land, IF THEY FIND OUT the
land is for sale next to them, they’re almost always HOT to buy it, then you’re up the creek!

AFTER YOU BUY THE LAND then talk to the neighbors, they often have info about your land that is PURE GOLD and very
valuable!

Does the land have water and trees? What kinds of trees? What kind of water, check for water rights. OLD GROWTH TREES
ARE PURE GOLD, ESPECIALLY CERTAIN SPECIES !

Inherited land for sale is PURE GOLD, the lazy fools that inherit are lazy city folk usually that likely NEVER looked at it. You can
often find PURE GOLD this way, I have !

Nobody considers a fence, but does your land have a fence on it? If yes, awesome, if its also a farm, that’s worth its weight in
GOLD!

Does the land have incredible access from many sides? If so then it can be subdivided into pieces and sold off making for
incredible profits. OFTEN IT IS THE CASE IF THE LAND HAS INCREDIBLE ACCESS, you can sell off a percentage of the land, and
KEEPS SOME SECTIONS FOR YOUR OWN, making your land FREE or even better!

What kinds of land are you buying? Certain scenic lands can be great passive income for campgrounds! A BOTTOMLESS
RENTAL INCOME, with basically NO UPKEEP COSTS!

People think RAW land requires upkeep, it DOES NOT in nearly all cases, nature takes care of the land! Stop worrying about
raw land upkeep, there really isn’t any!

FREE LAND YOU SAY? If there are valuable tree species of land on it, you can sell off part of the trees and get the land for FREE

What bounds the land? Good access or bad? Land locked is undesirable, but not always, are the neighbors shiftless losers with
dead cars in the front yard?

Does a GOVT. park surround your land? AWESOME, meaning NO neighbors to worry about in the future. This is very valuable.

Rich soil can be sold and is worth its weight in gold if you have deep rich soil.

How isolated is the land? How far away is the electric, how good is cell service? This is both very important and very valuable.

How far is the land from civilization? If far, this is both good and bad, some people desperately want this, others do NOT want
to travel far for gas and food. The best is the PERFECT golden middle between far and near.

Check for ZONING on the land and look out for pissy or crappy neighbors!

Realtors LOVE LOVE LOVE a quick sale, when you say “ALL CASH NO FINANCING” there is very little they love more than this!

Realtors WILL CONSIDER YOU OVER ANY OTHERS if you make it VERY VERY CLEAR that you mean business and are 100%
ready to drop CASH down and want the land, period.

The BEST WAY to get a deal on land is to GET IT BEFORE ITS LISTED, hound realtors to consider YOU before its listed and
make sure THEY KNOW you’ll take care of them if they consider you FIRST.

Realtors HATE HATE HATE getting banks involved in a sale, DROP the CASH, tell and show them you have the funds to make
their PAPERWORK few and EASY. They LOVE THIS!!

DO NOT BUY land locked land unless its both awesome and a good deal.

Is the land in a flood plane? Watch out for this, this can be both good and bad, if its EPIC LAND, the stupid people get scared
away from land like this.

DO MINERAL RIGHTS COME WITH THE LAND???? IF NOT YOURE BUYING THE TOP 1” OF LAND, AND NOTHING BELOW IT,
THIS IS VERY VERY IMPORTANT!

Everyone gets turned on by BARNS on land, and sheds, and trailers, but unless the barn is newish and VERY VERY NICE, these
really add next to NOTHING to the value of the land! BE WARNED OF THIS FACT!!!! DON’T GET DELUDED BY BARNS ON THE
LAND!

IF HOWEVER you have a barn on the land built with a CONCRETE FOUNDATION, this IS GOLD, since it can easily be turned
into a barndominum!

ALWAYS ALWAYS ALWAYS LOOK FOR RAW LAND NEXT TO OR VERY CLOSE TO RITZY NEIGHBORHOODS WITH NICE
HOMES! PURE GOLD 100% This adds INCREDIBLE VALUE to your land!

ALWAYS ALWAYS ALWAYS GET A TITLE SEARCH ON YOUR LAND BEFORE FINALIZING PAYMENT, THIS USUALLY COSTS
$500+

MANY IDIOTS ON THE WEB will tell you raw land is a risky investment and this is a TOTAL LIE. It only is if you act very
stupidly without knowing simple facts mentioned in this list! You can sell hunting rights, camping, trees etc. there are TONS of

Buy land with farming exceptions because this makes the lands yearly taxes VERY CHEAP! THIS IS WORTH ITS WEIGHT IN
GOLD, check your local PVA office for this.

ALWAYS ALWAYS ALWAYS WALK THE ENTIRETY OF THE LAND OR MOST OF IT BEFORE BUYING IT, LOOK FOR NASTY
DUMPING ON IT, THE GOOD, THE BAD, AND THE UGLY. Realtors almost NEVER EVER walk the land, they don’t have a clue

I’ve found PURE GOLD secrets on land and bought it because the person that inherited it NOR the realtor had a single clue

ALWAYS ALWAYS be cold hearted on the buy negotiation. Make an offer 15%+ or lower and offer CASH. Realtors work on
NUMBERS!!! They want to sell it off AS FAST AS POSSIBLE. Never ever EVER EVER offer full price on any land, no matter how
much you want it. THIS TACTIC WORKS ALMOST ALWAYS WHEN YOU HAVE CASH TO DROP ON THE PURCHASE!!
