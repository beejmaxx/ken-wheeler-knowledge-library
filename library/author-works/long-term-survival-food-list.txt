MAIN TIPS
Foods & oils that have DUAL or MULTI purpose use are very important to stockpile. Best of these are honey, salt, vodka,
ghee, and coconut oil.
There are MANY THINGS to store away for either safety, or SHTF, such as vitamins etc. but this is a list of JUST FOOD and
their immediate accessories.
Some foods are listed below as: THIS IS NOT LONG TERM STORABLE AND MUST BE ROTATE-EATEN! Meaning they
wont keep long term so you need to consume them as you go along and rotate NEW to replace them.
Some foods are listed below as: Store in chest freezer for maximum life. Meaning while they will store long term, they stay
nicer much longer if kept in a freezer, at least until and if the power goes out.
Its important to DIVERSIFY. Ask yourself if you could eat the same thing day after day after day after day if need be!
Other things OF COURSE ARE NEEDED; a way to protect your food and yourself, but THIS LIST is not ‘THAT LIST’. To
have something and being able to KEEP SOMETHING are of utmost consideration. Food is protected by LEAD & STRONG
WILL!
Quality SURVIVAL FOODS are NOT ABOUT WHAT IS MOST HEALTHY, BUT ABOUT SURVIVAL ITSELF. To “eat
good” is a luxury of a GOOD SITUATION. Supplementing GOOD FOODS with survival is important IF ONE CAN. A thin
spare tire is not meant to be driven on forever, but just to get you back home so you can change out the defective GOOD tire.
Pasta, rice, beans and some others NEED to be stored in airtight containers to keep out bugs. Repackaging certain foods is a
necessity such as bulk beans and powdered milk, also too to keep out humidity.
KEEP IT COOL & DRY!! MOST FOODS last a lot longer and taste better stored in a cool dry place. Especially seeds and
others this is IMPERATIVE! Keep things like you would wine, cool, dry, and in the dark.

COCOA POWDER
Enjoying a cup of hot chocolate post-apocalypse will actually be a possibility thanks to the fact that cocoa powder will last 30+ years.
Im talking about cocoa powder and not instant hot chocolate mix, which usually contains dairy.
JELLO MIX POWDERS
Jello mixes only stay good for about 3 years in the ORIGINAL packaging. My advice is to take the little packets out of the box and
vacuum pack them with the name of the flavor on the bag. Put the instructions in a file or binder after you cut them off the box.
GHEE
Ghee is very much like butter. When you boil butter and allow all of the moisture to dissipate you have ghee as a result. You can seal
it up and keep it in a cool and dry area and store it for a very long time.
COCONUT OIL!!
Coconut oil the best oil to stockpile, for a number of reasons. It is incredibly rich in nutrients and does all sorts of good for your body,
and it is solid at room temperature. This makes it easier to transport (should you need to) and minimizes the risk of leakage. You can
melt it easily in the palm of your hand or over a low cooking heat, and it is a much healthier oil to cook with that your traditional olive
oil. Extra virgin coconut oil also has a much higher shelf life of over 7 years, and has anti fungal and antibacterial properties – making
it a popular beauty product too. Yes, you may still want to feel pampered at the end of the world. It is not a cheap item, and it may feel
painful to buy in bulk at the time, but once SHTF you will certainly be glad of it. Shop for Extra Virgin Coconut Oil. ALSO TOO
coconut oil is a perfect skin moisturizer and keeps away ticks and mosquitos from biting.
