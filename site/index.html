THE PROJECT

Keep the text easy to search, the source easy to trace, and the permission status impossible to confuse.

01 / WHAT IT SOLVES

A corpus is more useful when its boundaries survive extraction.

Locally collected PDFs, lists, transcripts, and web links arrive with different authorship, rights, and extraction quality. This library preserves those distinctions instead of flattening them into one pile.

02 / IMPORTANT FEATURES

Plain files.
Rich provenance.

The repository favors durable research objects over a proprietary reader or database.

01

Page-preserving text

Markers such as ===== PAGE 0007 ===== make search results traceable to each source PDF sequence.

02

Checksum identity

Exact source hashes and original filenames identify files while keeping private machine paths out of the repository.

03

Evidence tiers

Primary material establishes what the author wrote. Discovery leads remain explicitly lower-confidence.

04

Rights-aware export

Public access is not treated as an open license, and restricted or unknown-rights material stays bounded.

03 / HOW IT WORKS

05 / STATUS & LIMITATIONS

Searchable now.
Careful about gaps.

Current corpus

Seventeen ingested records produce thirteen non-empty text exports, with thirty additional author-linked or discovered documents cataloged externally.

Extraction gaps

Three image-only PDFs need OCR. Their page placeholders are not transcriptions.

Rights boundaries

“Author-shared free download” records public access, not an inferred open-content license.

Claim boundaries

The archive documents sources. It does not independently validate scientific, historical, religious, health, or practical claims.

Visual material excluded

Original PDFs and local JPG references are represented through text metadata rather than republished binaries.

External links change

The broader discovery catalog points to third-party mirrors that may move or disappear.
